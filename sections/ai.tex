The use of AI in this paper is described in a few different ways. When it comes to the writing of the thesis
it was restricted to the following use:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Tikz Writing: Convert tables and diagrams from handwritten images to tikz syntax.
    \item Writing review: AI searched for grammatical inconsistencies, typos, open-ended and poorly-formed sentences.
    \item Review the mathematical argument: The AI was instructed to look for loops or failed arguments across mathematical proofs and derivations.
    \item Loop Review: Read and evaluate the work multiple times and provide critics and feedback about the overall writing.
    \item Latex Formatting: AI was used to adapt the paper to the necessary standards demmanded by a dissertation.
        This refers only to document structure(font size, title format, citations etc) not the writing content.
\end{itemize}
AI most relevant effort came in into the appendix for helping writing down a long list
of well stablished mathematical definitions to provide the reader the necessary background. 
Text outside definitions are not a product of AI use, AI only wrote the definitions itself.
The main body of the work (from introduction to conclusion) is almost entirely written by hand
having AI only as a default reviewer.

During the development and research of the thesis, AI was used as a learning gate
and searching mechanism to discuss ideas, look for references, investigate topics and understand
papers and works from others.
