\chapter{Rules of the underlying algebras}\label{sec:appendix-gla}

The derivations of Chapter~\ref{sec:gpa} and Chapter~\ref{sec:glp} rest on a
body of rules that belong to the linear and affine theories
$\mathbf{GLA}$ and $\mathbf{GAA}$ rather than to the inequality layer studied
here. They are established in
\cite{bonchi2017interacting,bonchi2019affine,paixao2022highlevel} and are
collected below for reference, grouped as they are cited in the text: (G1)
matrices, (G2) transposition, (G3) bending and (G4) the spider property of the
black structure.

Semantically the four groups say, respectively: that diagrams denoting linear
maps compose as matrices, so any such diagram may be replaced by a single box;
that reflecting a diagram left-to-right denotes the converse relation, and that
every linear relation factors as a cospan $E;F^{\mathrm{op}}$, i.e.\ as the
solution set of a system $Ev=Fw$; that an output wire may be bent into an input
wire and back, the white cap differing from the black one by an antipode; and
that a connected network of black nodes is determined by its number of inputs
and outputs alone.
